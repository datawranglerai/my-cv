Redspace mapping is a professional project created in partnership with the Principles for Peace foundation. It examines incendiary language in discourse about the Israel–Palestine conflict, with the aim of helping the foundation understand how peace initiatives might be communicated without inflaming the conversation.

## Why the language matters

In a conflict this sensitive, wording can carry different meanings and risks for different audiences. The project takes the language of the existing discourse as its subject, rather than treating communication as a final polish applied after an initiative has been designed. Its purpose is to give the charity a clearer view of where that discourse is most charged.

## My contribution

I worked on the discourse analysis in partnership with Principles for Peace. The mapping centres the language itself, giving the foundation a way to examine where messages may become incendiary.
