I wrote and maintain an open-source guide to self-hosting n8n on Google Cloud Run. It is for people who want to operate their own automation infrastructure on Google Cloud rather than rely only on a managed n8n deployment.

## A guide that people use

The repository has more than 600 GitHub stars. Members of the Google Cloud Run team use it and have collaborated on it, alongside other contributors. That involvement has helped make the guide a shared resource, while I remain responsible for maintaining it.

## The contribution

The guide gives readers a practical route through this deployment setup. I maintain the instructions and the open-source repository as people use and contribute to it.
