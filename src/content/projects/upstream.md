Upstream is an AI for Good project developed at Braidr in partnership with Fieldcraft Studios. We analysed the discourse around water use to look for ways of raising awareness of antimicrobial resistance in UK rivers beyond the people already talking about it.

## The communication problem

An issue can be urgent without being part of most people's everyday conversation. For this project, the question was how discussion of water might offer routes into a subject that otherwise stays within a narrow circle. Mapping the existing discourse helps identify language and contexts that could make the issue easier to encounter and understand.

## The work

I led the Braidr side of this AI for Good work, collaborating with Fieldcraft Studios. The analysis connects patterns in water-use discourse to possible ways of talking about antimicrobial resistance in rivers outside the current echo chamber.
