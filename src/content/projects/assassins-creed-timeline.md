I've been a fan of Assassin's Creed and its lore for years, but I could never keep track of which characters were doing what, when. This is my attempt to make that chronology something I can explore: an interactive historical atlas bringing the major character arcs and events together on one timeline.

## Keeping track of the story

The series moves between historical eras, intertwining characters' lives with the histories of the Assassins, the Templars and the Isu. Remembering an individual game's story is one thing; placing it alongside everything else is another. I wanted a way to see where those stories sit in time and follow the threads that run between them.

The atlas organises the chronology into parallel lanes for the Isu and cataclysms, Pieces of Eden, lives and legacies, and orders and traditions. Era navigation and filters for games, categories and characters give readers different ways into the same history. The timeline can be panned and zoomed, with individual memories available to explore.

## Data Is Beautiful meets Assassin's Creed

This is a passion project about making a fictional history readable through data visualisation. Character portraits, colour-coded themes and a shared time axis bring the lore into a visual form, so readers can move between the broad chronology and the people and events that interest them.

My starting point was a question I kept asking as a player: where does this fit? The atlas is my way of answering it, blending the curiosity of a long-time fan with the pleasure of making complex information easier to explore.
