Declassified Reclassified is a hobby project in which I read recently declassified US UAP files through a journalistic lens. I publish the analysis as GitHub Gists, keeping the work close to the documents that prompted it.

## The question behind it

Declassification makes records available, but it does not automatically make their meaning clear. My interest is in what the files actually say, what context is needed to read them, and where an attractive interpretation runs beyond the available record.

## The format

I publish that reading as GitHub Gists, writing for readers who may not have worked through the files themselves. The format lets me keep the analysis close to its sources and separate what a document says from what I make of it.
